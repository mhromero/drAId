A 47-year-old male presents with watery rhinorrhea, frequent sneezing, and nasal itching for the past 3 weeks. Symptoms worsen while cleaning the storage room and after exposure to dust.

History: seasonal allergic rhinitis since adolescence. Denies diagnosed asthma, although he has had occasional episodes of mild exertional dyspnea. No known drug allergies.

Examination: congested nasal mucosa, hypertrophic turbinates, clear lung auscultation without wheezing. Baseline SpO2 98%.

Plan: start cetirizine 10 mg once daily for 14 days and nasal saline irrigation 2 to 3 times per day. Follow-up in 2 weeks to assess response and possible need for intranasal corticosteroid.
